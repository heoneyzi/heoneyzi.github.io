LLM · RAG · Prompting · 2023–24

Persona chatbot

Chatting with a character through RAG and prompts

deep daiv. NLP team project: a Streamlit chat that speaks as an actor or a drama character, grounded in their wiki page through RAG.

Demo · final seminarTeam Lead · 4-member teamNov 2023 – Jan 2024

1Topic

Fan-chat services depend on the star actually replying, or on a model trained per person. Could a general LLM plus the right context play anyone?

2What I did

Led the team through its RAG design — first fetching the wiki section each question needs, then retrieval over the whole page with the Assistants API — and owned one persona’s prompt: drama lines as examples, first-person speech and rules that kept the character in role.

3Result

Retrieval kept the facts grounded — the actor’s persona turned down peaches because of the allergy on his page — while negative examples and injected date, weather and news made replies noticeably more natural (qualitative tests on four personas).

VisionWhy it matters to me

What a model says depends on the context we give it. Retrieval grounded each persona in facts from a real document, and the prompt shaped its voice — an early lesson in steering LLM behaviour, where every rule came from an observed failure.

— Jiheon Kang

01 · BackgroundThe question

Retrieval-augmented generation fetches relevant passages from a document — here the person’s profile page — so answers stay grounded in facts. Prompt engineering steers behaviour with instructions and examples. Neither changes the model’s weights, so a new persona costs one document, not one training run.

02 · ApproachHow it works

  1. Build retrieval (RAG)Start by fetching the wiki section a question needs; when page layouts proved too inconsistent, move to retrieval over the whole page with the Assistants API.
  2. Write one reusable promptGoal, background, rules, example lines and “false examples”.
  3. Fix what breaksAdd rules only after watching the character fail — e.g. mixing up the drama and the real world.
  4. Ship a demoServe it as a Streamlit chat for the final seminar.
