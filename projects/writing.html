Newsletter & Magazine

Explaining AI to everyone

Nine issues of the weekly newsletter 위클리 딥 다이브 and three pieces for magazine vol.0, under the pen name 에디터 져니 (“Editor Journey”).

Newsletter Writer · Magazine EditorWeekly AI Newsletter Writer · AI Magazine EditorAug 2024 – Jan 2026

1Topic

Research only matters if people can follow it. The deep daiv. Contents Team publishes a weekly AI newsletter and a printed magazine for newcomers.

2What I did

Wrote nine newsletter issues on multimodal AI (Mar 2025 – Jan 2026) and, as a magazine editor, three pieces for vol.0 (Aug – Dec 2024).

3Result

Several issues grew out of — or into — my own research: hallucination (#95, #114, #125) and video grounding (#109).

VisionWhy it matters to me

Explaining an idea in plain words is the best test of whether I understand it. I want my research to be readable by the people it is meant to help.

— Jiheon Kang

01 · BackgroundThe question

Each issue takes one or two recent papers — full-duplex speech models, missing modalities, LVLM hallucination, LLaVA, video grounding, watermarking, attention sinks — and explains them with everyday analogies for a general Korean audience.

02 · ApproachHow it works

  1. Pick one ideaOne frontier paper per issue, chosen for why it matters.
  2. Find the analogyExplain it through something everyday — an eyewitness, a smartphone, a highlight reel.
  3. Edit for newcomersFor the magazine: a concept primer, an interview and a first-coding guide.
