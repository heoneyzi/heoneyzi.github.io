Reasoning · Explainable QA · 2025

Bi-CoT

An AI that shows its reasoning — and checks it backwards

First-author paper accepted at the 6th Korea Artificial Intelligence Conference.

Accepted · 6th Korea AI ConferenceFirst author2025

1Topic

Questions such as “what office did the lead actress of film X hold?” need several facts in a row. If an early step is wrong, the error quietly spreads — and most systems show only the final answer.

2What I did

Designed an inference-only pipeline: split the question into sub-questions, answer each with frozen models, check every step against its evidence, then verify the chain in reverse.

3Result

On HotpotQA, exact match rose from 24.16 to 42.77 and F1 from 33.15 to 55.40 over a retrieve-and-read baseline — with no parameters trained.

VisionWhy it matters to me

Multi-hop QA is a task people designed. The habit it taught me — show each step and check it — is what I now bring to questions nature designed.

— Jiheon Kang

01 · BackgroundThe question

Strong question-answering systems are usually fine-tuned on the benchmark and return only an answer, so when they fail it is hard to see which step went wrong. Bi-CoT keeps every model frozen and changes only how inference is run: it records each step and adds a backward pass that can catch mistakes.

02 · ApproachHow it works

  1. DecomposeSplit the question into sub-questions that depend on each other.
  2. Answer with evidenceA frozen retriever and reader answer each step, and each answer is checked against its passage.
  3. Verify backwardsRe-check the whole chain from the answer back to the question before finalising.
