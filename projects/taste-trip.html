Recommender systems · Data · 2024

Taste Trip

A restaurant → café route recommender

deep daiv. recommender-systems team project, built from crawled Naver Place reviews.

Streamlit prototypeTeam LeadSummer 2024

1Topic

Map apps rank places one at a time, but a day out is usually a pair: a meal, then a café nearby.

2What I did

Led the team and built the three-stage Naver Place crawler (venues → reviews → reviewer histories), cutting a test run from 9 min 20 s to about 4 min 20 s.

3Result

A 51,705-user × 337-venue matrix, four recommenders (popularity, content-based, matrix factorisation, hybrid) and a demo that walks a user from a restaurant to a café.

VisionWhy it matters to me

Most of the work was data — collecting it, cleaning it, filling its gaps. That respect for the data pipeline carried straight into my biological projects.

— Jiheon Kang

01 · BackgroundThe question

Collaborative filtering learns from other people (“users who liked what you liked also went to…”); content-based filtering compares the places themselves, using review keywords and categories, so it works even for a brand-new user. With only 1.6 % of the matrix filled, sparsity was the central problem.

02 · ApproachHow it works

  1. CollectCrawl 337 venues (204 restaurants, 133 cafés) and their reviewers’ histories.
  2. Fill the gapsImpute sparse users from similar users before training.
  3. RecommendCompare popularity, content-based, matrix-factorisation and hybrid models.
  4. Walk the routeSix quick ratings, then a restaurant and a café in the same area.
